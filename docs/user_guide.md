# Trader User Guide: Goldman Sachs (GS) Directional & Insight Indicator (V2 Upgrade)

This guide instructs discretionary and systematic paper traders on how to interpret, monitor, and safely utilize the multi-output predictions generated by the GS AI trading indicator model (`model-v2`).

---

## 1. Indicator Outputs and Signal Interpretation

At the conclusion of each hourly trading candle, `src/insights.py` ingests closed bar data and emits seven distinct insight fields:

```json
{
  "direction": "Up",
  "confidence": 0.584,
  "horizon": "H1",
  "vol_class": "Normal",
  "band_low": -0.0041,
  "band_high": 0.0052,
  "regime": "Chop",
  "historical_hit_rate_for_this_hour_and_side": 0.562
}
```

### 1.1 Directional Action Signal (`direction`)
The operational decision rule in `model-v2` is **strictly two-sided**, driven by frozen out-of-fold quantile thresholds:
- **Up Signal**: Emitted when calibrated probability $\hat{p}_t \ge q_{\text{hi}}$ ($q_{\text{hi}} = 0.5632$ final refit).
  - *Trader Meaning*: Statistical upward bias for the subsequent 1-hour bar ($H1$).
- **Down Signal**: Emitted when calibrated probability $\hat{p}_t \le q_{\text{lo}}$ ($q_{\text{lo}} = 0.4343$ final refit).
  - *Trader Meaning*: Statistical downward bias for the subsequent 1-hour bar ($H1$).
- **No-Signal / Abstention**: Emitted when $q_{\text{lo}} < \hat{p}_t < q_{\text{hi}}$.
  - *Trader Meaning*: Market conditions are neutral, balanced, or uninformative. Abstain from entering new directional positions.

### 1.2 Displayed Confidence (`confidence`)
- For **Up** signals: $\text{Confidence} = \hat{p}_t$.
- For **Down** signals: $\text{Confidence} = 1 - \hat{p}_t$.
- Always reported as a value $\ge 50\%$, reflecting the probability supporting the indicated direction.

### 1.3 Volatility Forecast Class (`vol_class`)
- Derived from an XGBoost regressor predicting forward log range $\ln\left(\frac{High_{t+1}-Low_{t+1}}{Close_{t+1}}\right)$:
  - **Low**: Expected candle range below the 33rd percentile ($< 54\text{ bps}$). Indicates quiet, compressed trading.
  - **Normal**: Expected candle range between 33rd and 67th percentiles ($54\text{ bps}$ to $82\text{ bps}$).
  - **High**: Expected candle range above 67th percentile ($> 82\text{ bps}$). Suggests expanded ranges, elevated risk, and wider slippage.

### 1.4 Expected Price Bounds (`band_low`, `band_high`)
- Quantile regression models ($q=0.10$ and $q=0.90$) outputting expected percentage boundaries for next-bar return.
- On historical data, these bands cover approximately **73% to 80%** of all realized price excursions with an average bandwidth of ~83 bps.
- Useful for dynamic stop-loss placement and take-profit target anchoring.

### 1.5 Macro / Micro Regime Tag (`regime`)
- Categorizes current market behavior into **Uptrend**, **Downtrend**, or **Chop**:
  - `Uptrend`: Trailing 20-day return $> 0$ and 20-bar price efficiency ratio $> 0.167$.
  - `Downtrend`: Trailing 20-day return $< 0$ and efficiency ratio $> 0.167$.
  - `Chop`: Intraday efficiency ratio $\le 0.167$.
- **Key Empirical Finding**: On the Research Holdout, the indicator achieved its highest accuracy in **Chop regimes (58.30% accuracy, +2.28 bps net return after 5 bps friction)**. Mean-reversion signals are particularly robust in horizontal chop.

### 1.6 Historical Conditioning (`historical_hit_rate_for_this_hour_and_side`)
- The historical out-of-fold win rate for signals emitted during the specific time of day (e.g. 10:30 bar) and direction (Up or Down). Provides empirical base-rate grounding.

## 2. When NOT to Trust the Insight Outputs

Each output has specific operational boundaries and statistical blind spots. Traders must know when to discard or discount each field:

### 2.1 When NOT to Trust the Direction Signal (`direction`)
1. **Immediately Preceding Major Macro Announcements**:
   - FOMC interest rate decisions, CPI releases, Non-Farm Payrolls, and Fed Chair press conferences override all technical and peer correlations. Do not take signals 30 minutes before or 60 minutes after major macroeconomic releases.
2. **Earnings Release Days**:
   - GS quarterly earnings announcements cause massive non-linear gap risks that no technical indicator can anticipate. Discontinue trading on earnings day and the preceding afternoon.
3. **Strong Counter-Trend Rallies / Sell-offs**:
   - In explosive momentum breakouts (e.g. market-wide $+2\%$ index days), mean-reverting signals may fire prematurely against the trend.

### 2.2 When NOT to Trust the Volatility Class (`vol_class`)
1. **Unscheduled Breaking News & Flash Events**:
   - The volatility regressor relies on backward-looking 1-minute and 1-hour realized volatility. If an geopolitical event, bank failure, or regulatory enforcement action breaks mid-session, the model will output `Low` or `Normal` while realized volatility explodes.
2. **First Bar of the Day (09:30–10:30 ET)**:
   - Overnight order imbalances and opening cross auctions create structural volatility spikes that decay rapidly. Treat opening bar volatility classifications with caution.

### 2.3 When NOT to Trust the Expected Bounds (`band_low`, `band_high`)
1. **Heavy Outlier & Black Swan Moves**:
   - The quantile bands cover 80% of normal distribution outcomes ($q=0.10$ to $q=0.90$). By definition, **1 in 5 bars naturally breaches these bands**. They are NOT hard maximums or guarantees; they represent empirical expectation boundaries.
2. **Liquidity Voids & Half-Days**:
   - On pre-holiday half-days or during sudden market-wide halts, spreads widen and bounds can be blown through by wide prints.

### 2.4 When NOT to Trust the Regime Tag (`regime`)
1. **Trend Inflection Points & V-Bottoms**:
   - The `regime` classification utilizes a 20-day trailing return filter. When the market makes a sharp V-bottom reversal, the regime filter will classify the market as `Downtrend` for several days even as price rallies aggressively.
2. **Early Morning Regime Transitions**:
   - Efficiency ratios computed across 20 hourly bars bridge multiple overnight sessions. A large overnight opening gap can artificially inflate the efficiency ratio, triggering a temporary `Trend` tag on an otherwise choppy session.

## 3. Live Data Feed Requirements

1. **Live 1-Minute Microstructure Bars**:
   - The V2 engine requires 1-minute bars to compute intrabar features (`realized_vol_1m`, `up_minute_ratio`, `clv`, `signed_volume_ratio`, `last15_ret`). A live pipeline must buffer sixty 1-minute bars and aggregate them at the top of the hour.
2. **SIP Consolidated Tape vs. Free IEX Feed**:
   - The model is trained exclusively on consolidated **SIP data** across all US venues.
   - Using a free single-exchange feed (such as IEX) will under-report total volume by 10x–50x, distorting `rvol` and volume indicators. For reliable live deployment, subscribe to a consolidated SIP data provider.
3. **Session-Anchoring Discipline**:
   - All 1-hour candles must be strictly anchored to regular session open (09:30, 10:30, 11:30, 12:30, 13:30, 14:30, 15:30 ET). Non-session-anchored bars (e.g. standard 10:00 or 11:00 clock bars) violate model feature timing.

## 4. Operational Risk and Friction Management

- **Transaction Costs**: All research results assume **5.0 bps ($0.0005$)** per round trip. If the prevailing bid-ask spread on `GS` exceeds \$0.15 (~3 bps), abstain from execution.
- **Holding Period**: The model operates strictly on a **1-hour horizon ($H1$)**. Enter at bar close / next open, and exit strictly at the close of the subsequent bar.
- **Overnight Prohibition**: **NEVER hold overnight.** The 15:30–16:00 bar generates no signals. All positions must be liquidated before 16:00 ET.
- **Stop Loss**: Maintain an intraday catastrophe stop at **-50 bps (-0.50%)** to guard against flash crashes.
