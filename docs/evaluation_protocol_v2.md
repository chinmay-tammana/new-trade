# Evaluation Protocol: Quarterly Walk-Forward OOF & Single-Read Research Holdout (V2 Upgrade)

This document formalizes the validation and evaluation methodology for the Goldman Sachs (`GS`) AI Trading Indicator V2 upgrade.

---

## 1. Motivation and Lessons from V1 Failure

In the initial V1 architecture, the dataset was split into fixed chronological blocks: `Base` (2 yr), `Fine-Tuning` (1 yr), `Validation` (602 bars, Nov 2024–Mar 2025), and `Test` (896 bars).
This design produced a critical failure mode:
1. **Regime-Biased Parameter Tuning**: The V1 Validation block occurred during a prolonged post-election bull market rally. As a consequence, threshold tuning switched the Down side off completely ($p \le 0.44$ was never reached), emitting 119 Up signals and 0 Down signals.
2. **Out-of-Sample Collapse**: When evaluated on the unseen Test block, the Up-only indicator collapsed to 46.28% accuracy (net return -7.24 bps/signal after 5 bps friction).
3. **Misleading Baselines**: Reporting "Always-Up on the same candles" identically equaled the Up-only signal accuracy, masking the lack of alpha.

---

## 2. V2 Evaluation Architecture

The V2 upgrade eliminates single validation blocks and introduces two rigorous statistical mechanisms:

```
[================= 12,004 Pre-Holdout Bars (2018-10 to 2025-10) =================] [8 Gap] [== 1,872 Research Holdout Bars ==]
[-- 2 Yr Initial Train --][ Q1 OOF ][ Q2 OOF ][ ... ][ Q20 OOF ]
                          |------ Expanding Window (Walk-Forward) ------|
```

### 2.1 Expanding Window Quarterly Walk-Forward (OOF)
- **Span**: October 15, 2018 to October 14, 2025 (12,004 session-anchored 1h bars).
- **Initial Training Window**: First 2 chronological years (~3,500 bars).
- **Step / Test Horizon**: 1 calendar quarter (63 trading days $\approx 441$ hourly bars).
- **Purge / Embargo Gap**: 8 session bars between training and test chunks to prevent label leakage across multi-hour horizons ($H1, H3, HD$).
- **Total Folds**: 20 contiguous expanding quarters.
- **Pooled Out-of-Fold (OOF)**: Every pre-holdout bar from quarter 1 onward receives exactly one truly out-of-sample prediction generated by a model trained strictly on past history.

### 2.2 Pre-Registered Research Holdout
- **Span**: October 16, 2025 through November 9, 2026 (1,872 session-anchored 1h bars).
- **Embargo**: An 8-bar purge gap separates the pre-holdout period from the holdout.
- **Strict Single Read**: The Research Holdout is never accessed during feature selection, model tournament, hyperparameter tuning, horizon selection, or calibration. It is read **strictly once** in `src/evaluate_holdout_v2.py`.
- **Pre-Refit Evaluation**: All holdout metrics are generated by the model configuration selected on pre-holdout OOF before any final refitting.

## 3. Pre-Registered Decision Criteria

Before accessing the Research Holdout, five formal criteria were locked into `docs/problem_definition_v2.md`:

| Criterion | Description | Target Threshold | Verdict on Holdout |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Criterion A** | Pre-Holdout Pooled OOF Log-Loss | Beats Constant Prior ($0.69272$) | **MET (0.69279 for Ensemble vs 0.69345 for XGB)** |
| **Criterion B** | Holdout Signal Counts & Accuracy | $\ge 100$ per side; Acc $\ge 53\%$ with $p < 0.10$ | **NOT MET**: 848 Up, 574 Down ($\ge 100$); Acc **52.25%** ($< 53.0\%$), $p = 0.0417$. No edge shown at 53% threshold. |
| **Criterion C** | Drift-Adjusted Edge per Side | Both Up and Down beat respective base rates | **MET**: Up +1.59% vs base (51.65% vs 50.06%), Down +3.20% vs base (53.14% vs 49.94%) |
| **Criterion D** | Probability Calibration Quality | Holdout Expected Calibration Error $\le 0.05$ | **MET**: Holdout $\text{ECE} = \mathbf{0.0249}$ |
| **Criterion E** | Integrity Suite & Causality | Zero leaks, 100% causal checks pass | **MET**: All 7 integrity checks passed |

## 4. Signal Generation & Calibration

1. **Probability Calibration**: Class-balanced Platt scaling fitted on pooled pre-holdout OOF predictions. Balancing ensures $p=0.50$ represents a neutral directional forecast.
2. **Two-Sided Quantile Thresholding**:
   - $q_{\text{lo}} = \text{Quantile}(p_{\text{cal}}, c/2)$
   - $q_{\text{hi}} = \text{Quantile}(p_{\text{cal}}, 1 - c/2)$
   - Coverage $c$ swept over $\{0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.30, 0.40\}$; $c=0.30$ chosen based on maximum Wilson 95% lower bound of accuracy subject to $n_{\text{up}}, n_{\text{down}} \ge 150$.
   - Pre-holdout frozen thresholds: $q_{\text{lo}} = 0.49039, q_{\text{hi}} = 0.50844$.
3. **Mandatory Two-Sided Assertion**: Code strictly asserts $n_{\text{up}} > 0$ and $n_{\text{down}} > 0$ on the holdout.

---

## 5. Statistical Rigor

- **Confidence Intervals**: Wilson score intervals for binomially distributed accuracy metrics; 1,000-sample block bootstrap intervals for economic net returns.
- **Null Permutation Test**: 10,000 shuffles of direction signals preserving exact Up/Down counts to compute the exact empirical $p$-value against the null hypothesis of zero skill.
- **Regime Stratification**: Independent evaluation across macroeconomic regimes (SPY 60-day trend: Bull, Bear, Chop; intraday efficiency: Trend vs Chop).
