Audit: Explanation of the 7 vs. 8 Down Signals on IEX

Bar 2026-11-12T10:30:00-05:00 was classified as Down in the initial live DB backfill (P(Up) = 0.3892 ≤ 0.4343) but as No Signal in the offline pure IEX diagnostic (P(Up) = 0.4822).

Root Cause: The initial live database backfill computed trailing 20-day volume statistics crossing the freeze date (Nov 9). Comparing 18,000 IEX live volume against 500,000 historical SIP volume collapsed rvol by 40× to 0.036, which tripped XGBoost's low-volume bearish split. When volume history is homogeneous (either pure IEX or pure SIP), rvol is ~1.00, keeping this bar neutral (No Signal).
