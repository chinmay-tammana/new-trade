# Phase 2: Data Collection and Audit

======================================================================
PHASE 2: DATA COLLECTION AND AUDIT (src/data_fetch.py, src/audit.py) (IMPACT Step 3)
======================================================================
1. Credentials: read APCA_API_KEY_ID and APCA_API_SECRET_KEY from .env with python-dotenv. If missing, STOP and tell the user to create a free Alpaca account (paper trading is enough), generate API keys, copy .env.example to .env and fill it in. Never print the keys.
2. Availability test first: request 1 day of 1-minute GS bars from about 3 years ago (try up to 5 adjacent weekdays if one is a holiday) with feed=sip. If it fails or returns nothing, STOP and report the exact error. Do not silently fall back to yfinance.
3. Download 1-minute bars for GS, SPY, XLF with alpaca-py (StockHistoricalDataClient, StockBarsRequest, TimeFrame.Minute, adjustment=Adjustment.RAW, feed from config). start = now - history_years - history_buffer_days; end = now_utc - alpaca_end_lag_minutes. Download month by month, cache each chunk as data/raw/alpaca_1min/{symbol}/{YYYY-MM}.parquet, resumable, retry with backoff, respect rate limits. Never overwrite cached chunks unless --refresh.
4. Build 1h bars ourselves, session-anchored: convert to America/New_York, keep only regular-session minutes (09:30 <= t < 16:00); bar_start = 09:30 + 60min*floor((t - 09:30)/60min); open=first, high=max, low=min, close=last, volume=sum; drop bars with no minute data. This yields bars at 09:30, 10:30, ..., 15:30 (the last covers 30 minutes); half-days end earlier (for example last bar 12:30). Document that this is the convention a live aggregator must use. Save data/processed/bars_{symbol}_1h.parquet.
5. Audit (data quality only; no modelling decisions from Val/Test): results/logs/data_validation.txt and results/tables/audit_summary.csv covering duplicates and ordering; bars per day (list days != 7 and explain half-days); zero-volume bars; NaNs; high<low or close outside [low, high]; gaps between trading days beyond weekends/holidays; total rows per symbol (expect about 7,000 for GS); inner-join of SPY/XLF to GS and rows lost; top 10 extreme single-bar returns.
6. yfinance parity check on the overlapping most recent ~60 days: close correlation, mean absolute close difference in bps, median volume ratio. If the volume ratio is unstable, flag `rvol` for the ablation. Note the live-feed risk: SIP volume is consolidated, while a free live feed may be IEX-only.
7. docs/dataset_card.md: source (Alpaca Market Data, feed, terms for personal/research use), collection period, unit of observation (one 1h GS bar), target definition, size, class balance of Base only, grouping (trading day), privacy (none: public market data), possible target proxies (none intended), known issues (unadjusted prices: dividends appear as small overnight drops). docs/data_dictionary.md: every raw and engineered column with unit and dtype.
8. Define era boundaries (calendar dates and bar positions): t_end, y4_start = t_end - era_days, y3_start = t_end - 2*era_days; i3, i4 = first bar at or after y3_start, y4_start. Save to results/logs/split.json (completed in Phase 4).
9. Audit figures: bars per year, price history with era boundaries shaded, volume histogram, bars-per-day histogram, missingness summary (each with caption and interpretation).
CHECK: availability test passed; audit clean or every anomaly explained; about 7,000 GS bars; SPY/XLF aligned; parity result recorded; no data files tracked by git.
